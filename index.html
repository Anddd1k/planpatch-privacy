PlanPatch: Cat Café Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 (app version 1.0.0).

Developer: And1k

Contact: andharut2000@gmail.com

Summary

PlanPatch is a puzzle game for all ages. It has no account, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no server of its own. Your game progress stays on your device.

When you first open the game it asks in which year you were born. Only players who answer that they are adults can ever be shown ads. For children, for players who are not certain adults, and for anyone who chooses "Prefer not to say", the game shows no ads, does not start any advertising software and sends no advertising-related data.

The age question

  • You type your birth year on a keypad. The game uses it only on your device, once, to decide which band applies: adult, not adult or unknown ("Prefer not to say" or an invalid answer).
  • The year itself is not saved and not sent anywhere. Only the band is saved in the game's private storage.
  • Someone born in a year in which they might still be under 18 is treated as not adult.
  • The answer is not asked again. It is kept when you use "Reset progress". To change it you must clear the app's storage or reinstall the app.

Data stored on your device

Level progress (stars, best scores), settings (sound and music volume, vibration, reduce-motion), simple play statistics (levels solved, hints used, time played), the age band described above and, for adult players only, counters that limit how often ads appear. This is kept in the app's private storage. If Android backup is switched on for your device, Google may back it up to your Google account under Android's normal rules; the developer cannot see it. You can erase everything with Settings -> Reset progress (except the age band), by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling.

Advertising - confirmed adult players only (Google AdMob)

If you answered as an adult, the game can show (a) an optional rewarded video that you may watch for a bonus hint and (b) an occasional full-screen ad between levels, never in the middle of a puzzle. There are no banner ads. Ads are provided by Google (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Only for these players, when ads are requested, Google and its ad partners may collect and process, according to Google's documentation: the Advertising ID (a resettable identifier you can reset or delete in Android settings), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and OS information, app and ad interaction information, and SDK diagnostic information. Google uses it to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. The developer does not receive your personal data; the developer sees aggregated reports. See https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form before any ad is requested; no ads are requested until consent is resolved. If you do not consent, the game stays fully playable without ads. Where required, Settings -> Privacy choices lets you change your choice later.

Test ads. During closed testing the game shows Google's test ads only.

Children and players of unknown age

Players who are not confirmed adults see no ads. For them the Google Mobile Ads SDK is never initialised: no consent form, no ad request, no Advertising ID access, no ad-related network traffic. The game has no chat, no links out of the app, no purchases and no social features.

Permissions

VIBRATE (optional vibration, can be turned off); INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, AD_ID and a few permissions that the Google ads library declares for itself (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE). The internet and advertising permissions are used only for the ads shown to confirmed adults described above. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or files.

Sharing, retention, security

The developer does not sell data and shares nothing itself. Data processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's own policies and retention rules; traffic to ad servers uses HTTPS.

Your choices and rights

Reset or delete your Advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android settings (Settings -> Privacy -> Ads); use Privacy choices in the game's Settings where available; erase local data as described above; or uninstall. For data held by Google use Google's tools (https://myaccount.google.com/). For anything about the app, use the contact address above.

Changes

If the ad setup or data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.
